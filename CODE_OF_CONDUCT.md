# Code of Conduct

We want PeakUI to be a welcoming, constructive, and professional community. By participating, you agree to follow these standards.

## Our Standards

- **Be respectful** — treat everyone with respect, even when disagreeing.
- **Be constructive** — give feedback that helps improve the project.
- **Be inclusive** — welcome contributors from all backgrounds and experience levels.
- **Be focused** — keep discussions relevant to PeakUI and its users.
- **Be honest** — do not misrepresent your work, credentials, or intentions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks
- Spam, unsolicited promotion, or off-topic disruption
- Sharing private or sensitive information without consent
- Advocating or enabling illegal or harmful activity

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email **info@peakservices-inc.com**.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions or comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

## Scope

This code applies to all PeakUI community spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project-related communication channels.
